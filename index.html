What is being recorded?

This extension records your browsing and search activity only during a daily time window between 17:00 and 22:00 for the duration of this study.

During these time windows, you will always see that an episode is active. Nothing is recorded outside of these time windows, and you can pause recording at any moment through the side panel by clicking the pause button. While recording is paused, your browsing data will not be tracked or stored.

You can request the deletion of all of your data, parts of your data, or withdraw from the study entirely at any time during the four-day period by contacting the study team using the contact information below. In this case, your data will be deleted within one working day, and the extension will no longer record any browsing data.

Study data are transmitted to and stored in a secure research database hosted on a European server using Supabase. The browsing data are associated with a study participant ID rather than your name or email. Information that could directly identify you is stored separately from the browsing data.

The research data will be retained for a minimum of 10 years after completion of the study.

Access to identifiable or pseudonymous study data is limited to the primary investigators of the study who require access for the purposes of this research.

During an active episode, the extension records:

  • Websites you visit (domain and page address)
  • Page titles
  • Search queries you type into search engines
  • Timing of searches and the type of source (news, search, social media, etc.)
  • HTML snapshots of the websites you visit
  • If you interact with AI: the questions you ask one of the AI assistants (Grok, ChatGPT or Claude), and their replies

The extension never records:

  • Passwords or anything you type into password fields
  • Email contents
  • Private messages
  • Banking, payment, or health portal contents
  • Visits to adult websites
  • Any activity outside the study recording window
  • Any activity during the study recording window once you manually activate the pause button.

If you visit a sensitive site during an active episode, its address and contents are not recorded to our database. Sensitive information and websites includes health care and insurance providers, online banking sites, adult websites, email portals, as well as passwords or login fields. Our data only shows that a redacted visit occurred, but does not show which website was visited.
